What is included and excluded?

The model includes international transport, simplified road transport, coffee roasting and packaging manufacture. Frozen container shipping is modelled as a generic reefer baseline plus explicitly entered incremental electricity; there is no validated frozen-specific default. Identical agricultural production and processing are excluded from both pathways; so are farm-to-export handling, storage energy, customs, warehousing, retail, consumer travel, brewing, packaging disposal and refrigerant leakage outside the reefer transport factor, pre-freezing coffee, and cold-store energy before/after voyage. These omissions matter if evaluating an entire supply chain.

How are shipping emissions allocated?

Sea: TEU-km factor × route km × max(shipping volume / usable container volume, shipment mass / usable payload). A full-container toggle rounds up to whole TEUs. Air: tonne-km factor × route km × shipment tonnes, or a volumetric chargeable-weight proxy if selected. Roasted retail bags have more volume than green coffee. These are scenario estimates, not shipment-level GLEC certification.

Packaging and roasted coffee density assumptions

Green beans are modelled at 705 kg/m³, roasted at 301 kg/m³ (literature-based example, varying with roast). Effective packing efficiency: green sacks 68%, bulk roasted 58%, 1 kg retail cartons 45%, 300 g retail cartons 38%. Packaging mass per kg coffee: green sacks 0.015 kg, bulk roast 0.025 kg, 1 kg retail 0.045 kg, 300 g retail 0.075 kg. These packaging values are illustrative until measured.
