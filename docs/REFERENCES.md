# Research bibliography and source audit

Accessed / reviewed for this handover: **21 October 2026**. Public pages below are linked; original papers may require access. Do not assume website values are current after this date.

## Direct Barista Hustle references

1. **The Decision Tree, TDT 2.07 — Roasting.** https://www.baristahustle.com/lesson/tdt-2-07-roasting/ — cites roasting estimates of 0.19 kg CO₂/kg green (German industrial roast, originally Killian et al. 2013 based on PCF Pilotprojekt Deutschland 2008); 0.70 kg CO₂/kg green attributed to natural gas at Peruvian roaster (Franco and Bartl 2018; note combined milling+roasting 0.89); and 0.42 kg CO₂/kg green attributed to Thai roaster gas (Phrommarat 2019). **Used for V1 roasting factor**. Check original primary publications before treating as directly comparable.
2. **TDT 2.08 — Packaging.** https://www.baristahustle.com/lesson/tdt-2-08-packaging/ — quotes historical Tchibo 0.13 kg CO₂ per kg green for packaging, about 1.8% of cup footprint. Reference for contextual cross-check **not** V1's per-kg-packaging factor.
3. **TDT 2.09 — Transport and Distribution.** https://www.baristahustle.com/lesson/tdt-2-09-transport-and-distribution/ — sea versus air; Tchibo all transport/distribution just over 7% in its system including consumer travel. **Contextual only**, not a freight emissions coefficient.
4. **Roasting at Origin (Barista Hustle, 16 Oct 2021).** https://www.baristahustle.com/roasting-at-origin/ — employment, wages and value retained at origin. **Do not equate economic sustainability with carbon savings.**

## Research and accounting standards

5. **Franco, M., Bartl, K. (2018), Carbon Footprint of the Coffee Roasting Process Based on Two Technologies with Different Sources of Energy in Peru.** https://doi.org/10.14207/ejsd.2018.v7n3p90 — primary paper to read; an independent 2025 Peruvian research summary reports 0.318 vs 0.744 kg CO₂e per kg of coffee for solar-assisted versus electricity+LPG. Summary: https://cienciaclimatica.igp.gob.pe/entities/interpretation/a004b7cc-994d-4288-a860-66bb73560cef . **Check denominators, scope, technology and whether Arabica is established in original**. Do not mix with BH's 0.70 gas value without analysis.
6. **Smart Freight Centre, GLEC Framework (currently advertises v3.2).** https://smartfreightcentre.org/page/glec — methodology aligned to ISO 14083 for logistics emissions. **Methodological reference, not a provenance for V1's specific .0717 or .1423 factors.** Obtain exact trade lane and allocation factors.
7. **UK Government, 2026 GHG conversion factors** (published June 11, flat-file update July 31). https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greenhouse-gas-reporting-conversion-factors-2026 — freight conversion factors and methodology. **V1's air .6 is not yet verified against the tables.** Record whether uplift, non-CO₂, WTT and allocation are included.
8. **PCF Pilotprojekt Deutschland (2008), cited via TDT:** original Tchibo LCA; trace primary report for boundaries.
9. **Killian et al. (2013), cited via TDT:** coffee value-chain carbon footprint, secondary origin of historical 0.19 benchmark. Check study geography/process.
10. **Phrommarat (2019), cited via TDT:** Thai coffee roasting gas 0.42 benchmark; find full bibliographic information and original unit.

## Company-specific evidence

11. **Go Get Em Tiger.** https://gget.com/ — publicly describes Roasted at Origin, Prism and shifting value-added work toward origin. **Do not infer precise packing format, volumes, air freight, transit configuration, or emissions from marketing site.** The air-freight scenario was supplied by user and remains independently unverified.

## Primary evidence to collect

- 2026 UK air freight workbook **exact cell/row** and appropriate uplift/mode. Freight-only air cargo versus belly cargo separately.
- GLEC 3.2 or carrier-accounting methodology including load allocation, dry/refrigerated vessel factors, TTW/WTW and electricity/refrigeration.
- Measured Arabica bulk density, roast loss and carton/pallet stowage for relevant beans; trace primary 705/301 kg/m³ study before citation.
- Roasting fuel/kWh records for typical roaster capacities, afterburners and grid factors at origin/destination.
- Actual sea routes and airport paths (not geodesic multiples).
- Actual material/weight of coffee retail bags, liners, cases, pallets and wrapping; embodied emissions factor by material.
- GGET-origin-roasted sample logistics, if a representative operational comparison is intended.

**Citation policy:** source or explicitly mark illustrative every numeric claim. Public-facing comparisons must show provenance and limitations.
